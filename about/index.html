MY STORY

A clear aspiration.
A growing perspective.

I am Jaithra Salem Chandrasekharan, a first-year Honours Health Sciences (Co-op) student at the University of Waterloo and an International Baccalaureate Diploma Programme alumnus.

My interest in medicine has grown through experiences in community pharmacy, hospital volunteering, and public health outreach. Supporting people in these settings has shown me how important it is to communicate clearly, stay attentive, and respond with compassion.

At Peel Memorial Centre, I help patients and visitors navigate an unfamiliar environment. Outside the hospital, my food security and community service work has drawn my attention to the everyday conditions that affect people’s well-being.

I am also curious about how technology can support care. Student robotics projects and school technology clubs gave me opportunities to explore ideas, collaborate, and learn from others.

My goal is to become a medical professional who combines scientific understanding with the patience to listen and the commitment to serve.
